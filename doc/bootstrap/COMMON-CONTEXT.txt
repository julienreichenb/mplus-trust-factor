M+ TRUST FACTOR — SHARED PRODUCT CONTEXT
========================================

1. Product mission
------------------
Build a website and later a World of Warcraft Retail addon that helps Mythic+ group leaders
distinguish between applicants who look similar in the in-game Group Finder.

Today the LFG applicant list mainly exposes:
- Class/spec/role information.
- Item level.
- Mythic+ rating.

Those values do not reliably show whether a player:
- Produces competitive damage or healing for their context.
- Survives difficult mechanics.
- Uses defensives correctly.
- Interrupts and crowd-controls relevant enemies.
- Uses dispels, purges, externals, and class utility.
- Has earned their score legitimately instead of being repeatedly carried or boosted.

The product computes an explainable, versioned "Trust Factor" from public data.

2. Target audience
------------------
- All Mythic+ players are technically in scope.
- Primary niche: approximately the best 5% of players who frequently pug with strangers.
- The system must not structurally punish non-meta specializations merely for being non-meta.
- A well-played non-meta specialization should be able to outrank a poorly played meta specialization.

3. MVP scope
------------
- Game: World of Warcraft Retail only.
- Region: EU only for MVP.
- Data model and APIs: region-aware from day one for future US, KR, TW, and other supported regions.
- Website:
  - Search a character by region, realm, and name.
  - Public character profile with overall Trust Factor and detailed dimensions.
  - Radar/spider chart.
  - Data-confidence indicator.
  - Red flags.
  - Last analyzed run and highest analyzed run, deduplicated if they are the same.
  - Historical season context.
  - Comparison of 2–10 candidates with overlaid radar charts and deltas.
  - Admin configuration for scoring weights, thresholds, and model versions.
- Addon:
  - Overall grade only in the LFG list when technically safe.
  - Tooltip with numeric score, confidence, freshness, and high-level red flags.
  - Static dataset generated outside the game and shipped with addon updates.
  - No live HTTP calls from Lua.
- No social reputation, player reviews, or leaver reports in the MVP.
- No attempt to infer detailed dungeon routes or skips in the MVP.

4. Eligibility for scheduled indexing and addon export
------------------------------------------------------
Eligibility is configurable. Initial policy:
- At least 20 known Mythic+ runs in the current season; AND
- Either:
  A. The character has timed at least the current season's dungeon count at or above the
     configured baseline level (default equivalent of all 8 dungeons at +10 when the season
     has 8 dungeons), OR
  B. The character belongs to the top 25% regional Mythic+ score population.

Do not hardcode "8" as a permanent game rule. Use `season_dungeon_count`.
Characters explicitly searched on the website may be ingested even when ineligible, but:
- They may be excluded from the daily addon dataset.
- Low-volume profiles must receive reduced confidence and a visible warning.

5. Scoring scopes
-----------------
The system must be capable of representing:
- Character overall.
- Role: DPS, tank, healer.
- Class.
- Specialization.
- Claimed Battle.net account-wide context.

MVP display:
- Website primarily shows character + current specialization/role context.
- In-game addon shows one overall character grade.
- Account-wide aggregation is only authoritative for users who later claim/link their Battle.net account.
- Unclaimed account links may only be "probable", never asserted as certain.

6. Score outputs
----------------
Public outputs:
- Trust Factor: 0–100.
- Grade: S / A / B / C / D.
- Data confidence: 0–100%.
- Dimension scores: 0–100.
- Authenticity score: 0–100.
- Red flags and positive context tags.
- Scoring model version and calculation timestamp.

Initial configurable grade thresholds:
- S: >= 90
- A: >= 80
- B: >= 65
- C: >= 50
- D: < 50

These are provisional product defaults, not scientifically validated cutoffs.

7. Core dimensions and initial weights
--------------------------------------
Initial default model, fully configurable and versioned:
- Performance: 32%
- Survival: 27%
- Utility: 23%
- Experience and consistency: 13%
- Mythic raid signal: 5%

Initial skill formula:
SkillScore =
  0.32 * Performance
+ 0.27 * Survival
+ 0.23 * Utility
+ 0.13 * ExperienceConsistency
+ 0.05 * MythicRaid

Authenticity adjustment:
ObservedTrust = SkillScore * (0.60 + 0.40 * AuthenticityScore / 100)

Confidence shrinkage:
FinalTrust =
  Confidence * ObservedTrust
+ (1 - Confidence) * 50

Where Confidence is represented internally as 0.0–1.0 in the formula.
All formulas, weights, thresholds, and metric definitions must be stored in model configuration,
not buried as unchangeable constants.

8. Dimension intent
-------------------
Performance:
- Compare performance against equivalent specialization, dungeon, and key-level brackets when possible.
- Raw DPS/HPS alone is not meaningful enough.
- Use current-context rankings plus consistency and contextual contribution.
- Never compare a support-heavy or tank/healer profile solely using raw damage.

Survival:
- Death frequency and timing.
- Avoidable damage where a curated mechanic catalog exists.
- Defensive cooldown use around dangerous events.
- Deaths while appropriate defensives or consumables were apparently available.
- Healthstone/healing potion usage when observable.
- Do not classify mandatory damage, intentional soaks, or role mechanics as avoidable without a rule.

Utility:
- Successful interrupts.
- Priority casts stopped.
- Useful crowd control.
- Dispels and purges.
- External defensive/support cooldowns.
- Class-specific utility.
- Raw cast count is not enough; relevance and timing matter where data permits.

Experience and consistency:
- Number and breadth of current-season runs.
- Repetition at high key levels.
- Recent progression.
- Previous-season score normalized to that season.
- Longer-term class/spec/role consistency.
- Old seasons have decreasing weight.

Mythic raid:
- Mythic raid progression.
- Aggregate Mythic parse signal for killed bosses.
- Low total weight.
- No detailed raid death analysis in the MVP.

9. Detailed run policy
----------------------
For each evaluated character:
- Analyze aggregated season data where available.
- Perform expensive detailed log analysis for:
  1. The latest public Mythic+ run.
  2. The highest-level or highest-value public Mythic+ run.
- If both refer to the same run, analyze it once.
- Cache immutable report data by report revision.
- Missing/private logs reduce confidence and may create a "Logs hidden/incomplete" red flag.
- Missing logs do not automatically force a score to zero.

10. Boost/authenticity policy
-----------------------------
The MVP should be intentionally aggressive at detecting suspicious patterns, but wording must remain probabilistic.

Potential suspicion signals:
- Sudden jump in key levels without intermediate progression.
- Best-scoring runs concentrated in a very short time window.
- Very low run volume compared with achieved score.
- Weak personal performance in the exact runs producing the score.
- Repeated grouping with the same substantially higher-rated players.
- Several stronger players appearing together in most top runs.
- High death rate or very low contribution in top runs.
- High score achieved almost exclusively inside one stronger premade.
- Unusually large divergence between rating and performance percentiles.

Mitigating signals:
- Confirmed high-rated main on the linked Battle.net account.
- Probable reroll with strong evidence.
- Prior-season high performance on the same class/role.
- Strong personal performance in the supposedly suspicious runs.
- Similar progression across multiple independent groups.
- Established history at the same level.

Suggested public tags:
- Boost suspected
- Atypical progression
- Logs hidden
- Insufficient data
- Low run volume
- Confirmed reroll
- Probable reroll
- Data stale

Never output "This player bought a boost" as a fact.

11. Missing data and explainability
-----------------------------------
- Calculate with available data.
- Track coverage per metric, dimension, and source.
- Shrink low-confidence scores toward neutral rather than toward zero.
- Show sources, freshness, model version, and dimension weights.
- Explanations should state major positive/negative contributors without exposing an easily gamed
  event-by-event formula.
- Every score snapshot must be reproducible from immutable inputs and a model version.

12. Monetization readiness
--------------------------
The launch can expose all details publicly, but the architecture must support future entitlements:
- Public summary score and grade.
- Premium detailed dimensions.
- Premium run-level details.
- Premium account-wide scopes.
- Premium custom scoring profiles.
- Premium expanded comparison.

Implement entitlement checks server-side. The frontend must not be the security boundary.
Do not implement payments in this MVP.

13. Data and ethics
-------------------
- Use public game data and public logs only unless a user later explicitly authorizes private access.
- Respect provider terms, rate limits, attribution, and removal/privacy mechanisms.
- Keep source payloads only as long as needed.
- Do not scrape.
- Provide a way to mark a score as stale or recompute it.
- Build a future-ready dispute/review record, even if no public dispute workflow is shipped in MVP.
